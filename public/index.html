FAQ

Questions creators ask

Is it free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it's free and there's no sign-up. You get a private edit link for your store; bookmark it to come back.

Does Sell Sphere take payments?

Not in this version. Each product's button goes to your own payment link (Razorpay, Instamojo, UPI page and so on), otherwise to a WhatsApp chat with you.

Do you read my Instagram?

No. We don't log in to or scrape Instagram. You can upload screenshots of your own profile and the AI reads only what's in them; the images aren't stored.

Are the prices right for me?

They're suggestions with a reason and a typical range for each format. You can change any price, or ask the AI for a new one.

Can I take my store offline?

Yes. Unpublish any time; the link stops working within about 30 seconds and you can republish later on the same link.
