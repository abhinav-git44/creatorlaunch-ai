FAQ

Questions creators ask

Do I need an account?

No. You get a private edit link for your store; bookmark it to come back.

Does Sell Sphere take payments?

Not yet. Each product's button goes to your own payment link (Razorpay, Instamojo, a UPI page and so on), otherwise to a WhatsApp chat with you.

Are the prices right for me?

They're starting points with a reason and a typical range for each format. Change any price, or ask the AI for a new one.

Can I write in Hinglish or Hindi?

Yes. Describe yourself however you speak; the store can be in English, Hinglish or Hindi.

Do you read my Instagram?

No. We don't log in to or scrape Instagram. You can upload screenshots of your own profile and the AI reads only what's in them; the images aren't stored.

Can I take my store offline?

Yes. Unpublish any time; the link stops working within about 30 seconds and you can republish later on the same link.
